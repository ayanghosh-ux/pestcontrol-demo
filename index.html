Field Knowledgebase

Expert Prevention Tips

Actionable physical and environmental protocols to protect your home against infestation.

Maintain at least a 6-inch clearance gap between soil/mulch and the bottom edge of wooden wall framing. Route AC condensation drains at least 5 feet away from exterior foundation walls.

Chief Entomologist Insight: Even a tiny plumbing leak under bathroom tiles can sustain an entire colony of 500,000 termites indefinitely without them ever needing outdoor soil.
Ask Advice on WhatsApp

In hotels, never place open suitcases on beds or carpeted floors. When returning home, unpack in a tiled bathroom and immediately wash travel apparel at 60°C (140°F) or tumble dry on high heat for 30 minutes.

Chief Entomologist Insight: Standard aerosol bug sprays do not kill bed bug eggs and often scatter nymphs into adjacent rooms through wall electrical sockets.
